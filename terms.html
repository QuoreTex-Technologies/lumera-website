2. The Service

Lumera is an interview integrity monitoring platform that helps hiring organisations detect AI-assisted cheating, remote-access fraud, and behavioural anomalies during remote job interviews. The Service consists of:

  • A lightweight desktop agent installed on the candidate's machine during an interview session
  • A browser extension for WebRTC-based signal collection
  • An HR dashboard for managing sessions and reviewing integrity reports
  • An AI analysis engine that processes signals and generates risk scores
