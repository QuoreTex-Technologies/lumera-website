DPDP Act Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023  ·  Updated: 14 May 2026
Lumera was designed from the ground up to comply with the DPDP Act 2023 — India's primary data protection law. This page explains how each key obligation is met. For enterprise customers, we provide a full Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request.

✓ Lawful purpose

Data is processed only for the stated purpose of interview integrity verification. No secondary use.

✓ Consent before collection

Candidates see a plain-language consent notice and must accept before the agent activates.

✓ Data minimisation

We collect only what is strictly necessary for fraud detection — no files, keystrokes, or browsing history.

✓ India data residency

All personal data is stored and processed in the AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. No cross-border transfer.

✓ Right to erasure

Candidates can request deletion of their session data. Requests are fulfilled within 72 hours.

✓ Automatic deletion

Session data is automatically deleted after 90 days unless a longer retention period is agreed in writing.

Role definitions under the DPDP Act

Data Fiduciary: The hiring organisation using Lumera. They determine the purpose of interview monitoring and are responsible for obtaining candidate consent.

Data Processor: Quoretex Technologies. We process personal data on behalf of the Data Fiduciary solely to provide the Lumera service, under the terms of our Data Processing Agreement.

Data Principal: The candidate whose data is collected during the interview session.

Consent notice

Before the Lumera agent activates, the candidate is presented with a consent notice that states, in plain language:

  • The name of the hiring organisation conducting the interview
  • Exactly what signals are monitored (process names, Bluetooth devices, face count, voice activity, network hostnames)
  • What is explicitly NOT monitored (screen content, files, keystrokes, audio recordings)
  • How long the data is retained and how to request deletion
  • Contact details for data-related queries

Consent is revocable at any time before the session begins.

Grievance Officer

As required by the DPDP Act, Quoretex has appointed a Grievance Officer for data protection matters:

Name: Available to customers and candidates upon request
Email: lumera@quore-tex.com
Response time: Acknowledgement within 24 hours, resolution within 15 business days

Enterprise DPA

Enterprise customers requiring a formal Data Processing Agreement (DPA) can request one by emailing lumera@quore-tex.com. The DPA covers sub-processor details, security obligations, breach notification timelines, and audit rights.
