Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 14 May 2026  ·  Version 1.0
Short version: Lumera only collects data that is directly necessary to detect interview fraud. We never record personal files, browsing history, or keystrokes. All data is stored in India (Mumbai AWS region) and is deleted after 90 days unless you request otherwise.

1. Who we are

Lumera is an interview integrity product operated by Quoretex Technologies, a company incorporated in Maharashtra, India. Our registered address is Amravati, Maharashtra. Contact: lumera@quore-tex.com.

2. What data we collect

From candidates (during an active interview session only)

  • Process names Running process names scanned for known AI tools and remote-access software (e.g. "TeamViewer.exe", "Cluely.app"). We never collect process arguments, file contents, or personal data from process memory.
  • Bluetooth device names Names of paired Bluetooth devices (e.g. "AirPods Pro"). We do not access Bluetooth audio streams or content.
  • Network hostnames Domain names of active network connections to detect AI API calls (e.g. "api.openai.com"). We do not capture packet contents, passwords, or browsing history.
  • Hardware signals Monitor count, USB device type (storage/audio), screen resolution. No screen content is recorded.
  • Behavioral signals Tab-switch events, window-focus duration, face presence (face count only — not facial recognition), gaze deviation angle, and typing activity timestamps.
  • Voice metadata Voice activity detection (speaking / not speaking), voice consistency score (a hash — never a recording stored as audio), and ambient sound level.
  • Video frames Low-resolution JPEG frames captured every 30 seconds for face count verification. Frames are encrypted in transit and at rest. Stored for 30 days, then deleted.

From HR users (dashboard)

  • Name, work email, and company name collected at registration.
  • Session audit logs (who created which session, timestamps).
  • Browser type and IP address for security logging.

What we do NOT collect

  • File system contents or personal documents
  • Browsing history or URL contents
  • Keystrokes or clipboard contents (clipboard event count only)
  • Audio recordings (voice signals are reduced to metadata hashes)
  • Biometric templates or facial recognition data

3. How we use data

Interview session data is used solely to generate an integrity report for the hiring organisation. We do not sell, share, or use candidate data for profiling, advertising, or model training without explicit opt-in consent.

4. Data residency and storage

All data is stored in the AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region. No data is transferred outside India. Session data is retained for 90 days and then deleted unless the customer requests a longer retention period in their enterprise contract.

5. Candidate rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, candidates have the right to:

  • Access a summary of data collected about them during a session
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of their session data
  • Withdraw consent before the interview session begins

To exercise these rights, email lumera@quore-tex.com with the subject "Data Rights Request" and the interview session reference number.

6. Consent

Candidates must explicitly accept the Lumera monitoring consent notice before the agent activates. The consent notice clearly states what is monitored and for how long. Candidates may decline, in which case the agent does not activate (browser-only monitoring may still apply at the hiring organisation's discretion).

7. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. Video frames are AES-256 encrypted at rest. Access to session data within the Lumera platform is role-based and audited. We conduct annual penetration tests and maintain SOC 2 Type II readiness. Contact lumera@quore-tex.com to request our security documentation.

8. Changes to this policy

Material changes to this policy will be communicated by email to registered HR users at least 14 days before they take effect. The current version is always available at this URL.
